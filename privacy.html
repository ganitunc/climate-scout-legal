Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 6, 2026

Climate Scout Pro is a travel planning and weather risk mitigation app developed by Gani Tunc.

Data We Collect

Climate Scout Pro does not require account registration and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, exact location, photos, contacts, or messages.

Local App Data

The app may store certain data locally on your device, such as recent searches, selected language, packing list items, pinned trip information for widgets, and subscription status. This data is used only to provide app functionality.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Open-Meteo to retrieve weather and climate data. The app may also use Apple services such as MapKit or location search to help find destinations. These services may process technical data such as search queries, IP address, or device-related information according to their own privacy policies.

Subscriptions and Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. Climate Scout Pro does not receive or store your payment card details.

Tracking and Advertising

Climate Scout Pro does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not track users across apps or websites.

Children’s Privacy

Climate Scout Pro is not directed to children under 13.

Contact
