2. What the app does

The app analyses historical weather records for a destination and a date range and presents averages, probabilities, activity suitability scores, packing suggestions and destination comparisons.

3. This is not a forecast

The app describes what the weather has done in past years on the same calendar dates. It does not predict what the weather will do. Actual conditions will differ, sometimes substantially.

Do not rely on the app for decisions where weather affects safety — mountaineering, sailing, aviation, diving, severe-weather planning or similar. Use official meteorological services and current forecasts for those. The packing lists and activity scores are convenience suggestions, not professional advice.
